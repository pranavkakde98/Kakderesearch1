You bring the decision. We research what it actually rests on.

Fixed-scope strategic projects for investors, private capital and businesses deciding where to commit capital or strategy across India and the emerging markets. Each project is scoped in writing before work begins, and the ones where independent research would not improve the decision are declined.

Project families

Four shapes of work

Consulting is strategic research applied to one decision — not execution support and not personalised securities advice. Timing depends on scope and on the size of the comparison set: multi-country primary-source work is quoted with a realistic date, not a fixed turnaround.

Comparative market study Market-entry & expansion study Sector & value-chain assessment Research sprint / decision memo
01

Comparative market or country study

Which market deserves the commitment, measured against the real alternatives.

What it answers

A like-for-like comparison of the markets competing for a defined allocation or strategy — India against Indonesia, Vietnam, Brazil, Mexico, or the set the decision actually names — on growth, valuation, currency, access and investability.

Typical output

A written study on a fixed peer and metric set, with a reproducible data appendix and a walkthrough. An investment-committee presentation can be added as a delivery option.

Source standard

Primary and official data, each figure carrying its source and date; judgement labelled as judgement.

Excludes

No pre-set winner and no recommendation to buy or sell a security. Where a market ranks badly, the study says so.

02

Market-entry & expansion study

Whether, where and how to enter or expand — on evidence, not on the pitch.

What it answers

Market sizing, demand structure, competitive and regulatory conditions, and the route in — for a corporate or investor weighing a specific entry or expansion, with the comparison set named at the outset.

Typical output

A written study against the agreed scope, a risk register grounded in evidence rather than generic caution, and a sources-and-data appendix.

Source standard

Official statistics, filings and primary interviews where scoped; assumptions stated plainly enough to be challenged.

Excludes

Not transaction, legal or tax execution. The study informs the decision; the decision and its execution stay with you and your advisers.

03

Sector, value-chain or China+1 assessment

Who captures the value in a supply chain, and where the exposure actually sits.

What it answers

A sector or value-chain analysis — including China+1 relocation questions — tracing where margin and value accrue across the chain, and which market and which participants capture it.

Typical output

A written assessment mapping the chain, the comparators and the exposure, with data appendix and walkthrough.

Source standard

Trade, production and company data; primary sources where the question warrants them.

Excludes

No assumed conclusion. A relocation thesis is tested against the alternatives, not presumed.

04

Research sprint / decision memo

One defined question, answered fast and in writing, ahead of a decision.

What it answers

A single, well-defined question — a policy change, a currency move, a valuation puzzle, a scenario — researched and written to be forwarded to a committee, not summarised from a call.

Typical output

A short written memo with charts and a data appendix, and one round of written follow-up.

Source standard

The same evidence discipline as a full study, scaled to one question.

Excludes

No recommendation to buy or sell, and no personalised regulated investment advice.

How a project works

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    Thirty minutes to establish the decision, the markets it touches and what a useful answer must contain. If the question cannot be answered properly, you are told on this call.

  2. 02

    Written scope

    The question, the comparison set, the outputs, the source standard, the exclusions and a realistic delivery date, agreed in writing before work starts.

  3. 03

    Research and analysis

    Primary and official sources pulled and cleaned in-house and connected into one argument.

  4. 04

    Delivery and walkthrough

    A written report with the reasoning and the data shown, a walkthrough call, and one round of revision. A committee presentation is available as an option.

Independent research, not execution.

Consulting is fixed-scope strategic research. It does not sell or distribute financial products, take custody of assets, execute or arrange transactions, or provide personalised regulated investment advice. It narrows the uncertainty around a decision; it does not remove it, and it promises no outcome. That independence — no execution, no product, no external capital — is what lets a conclusion be inconvenient.
