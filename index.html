Where the work is

Founded 2025 · Based in India · Working globally

One team, every market.

Kakde Research works from India for clients across the world’s leading financial centres, in North America, Europe, the Gulf and East and South-East Asia. There is no twenty-year archive here and no network of offices, and we do not pretend otherwise. Can Global Investors Actually Capture India’s Economic Rise?

A dollar invested in India captures part of India’s rise — materially less than GDP growth implies. Return depends on corporate earnings, valuation, dilution, investability and currency translation. India is structurally attractive but valuation-sensitive, and it has to be sized against the markets competing for the same capital. An India-centred question, answered inside a genuine comparative emerging-markets framework.

How the work is done

Six commitments · every engagement

  • Original research

    Official data, filings and primary sources, pulled and cleaned in-house. Where a series is revised or interrupted, that is recorded rather than smoothed over.

  • An explicit comparison set

    Every conclusion is tested against the alternatives the decision actually runs between. The peer set is chosen for the question and written into the scope, whether it spans emerging markets, developed anchors or both.

  • Sources and dates

    Every figure carries its source and its date, and the data appendix is written so that your own team can rebuild it.

  • Judgement labelled as judgement

    Where the evidence runs out and interpretation begins, the report says so, and states the conditions that would change the view.

  • Direct access

    You speak to the people doing the work. The founder scopes every engagement, reviews it and stays on the relationship; the analysts are reachable rather than hidden behind an account layer.

  • Independence

    Consulting and research fees are the only revenue: no brokerage, no execution, no products, no asset-management incentive. A conclusion is allowed to be inconvenient.

The founder

Pranav Prakash Kakde

Founder & lead researcher
Based in India · working globally

Founder-led. You speak to the people doing the work.

Before starting Kakde Research I worked in London, at a global investment bank and at a financial-data company, across macro and market data. I trained as a computer engineer before an MSc in Finance, and the data pipelines behind the work are still built in-house rather than lifted from a vendor summary.

The practice exists because important investment and strategic decisions are so often made from generic research, inherited assumptions or evidence assembled a long way from the decision itself. The founder scopes every engagement, directs and reviews the work and stays on the relationship; a team of six analysts assembles, checks and maintains the evidence. Founder review on every deliverable caps how much runs at once, and the limit is deliberate.
