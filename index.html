Emerging Markets Desk

01 · Retained desk · Comparative research

You may not need an internal emerging-markets research team. You may need access to one.

Most organisations with exposure across several emerging markets cannot justify an economist, a strategist and a data analyst for each. The questions still arrive — before the committee, after the shock, when a market moves and someone has to say what it means for the others.

The Emerging Markets Desk is that capability, retained: continuous comparative research across the markets relevant to you, built around your questions rather than a generic country feed, delivered in whatever form the decision needs.

Can Global Investors Actually Capture India’s Economic Rise?

A dollar invested in India captures part of India’s rise — materially less than GDP growth implies. Return depends on corporate earnings, valuation, dilution, investability and currency translation. India is structurally attractive but valuation-sensitive, and it has to be sized against the markets competing for the same capital. An India-centred question, answered inside a genuine comparative emerging-markets framework.

Why Kakde Research

Six commitments

  • A comparative lens

    Every market conclusion is tested against the alternatives competing for the same allocation or the same plant. The peer set is chosen for the question, not inherited from a benchmark.

  • Deeper India capability

    Emerging markets are the opportunity set. India is our deepest specialist market: read from India, continuously, to institutional standard.

  • Original research

    Official data, filings and primary sources pulled and cleaned in-house. Where a series is revised or interrupted, that is recorded, not smoothed over.

  • Direct analyst access

    The researcher you speak to is the researcher who does the work. No account manager, no junior team separated from your question.

  • Transparent method

    Sources dated, judgement labelled as judgement, and the conditions that would change a conclusion stated alongside it.

  • Independence

    Research and consulting and nothing else: no brokerage, no execution, no product distribution. Nothing earns more when a conclusion goes one way.

The founder

Pranav Prakash Kakde

A researcher, not a research department.

Before starting Kakde Research I worked in London, at a global investment bank and at a financial-data company, across macro and market data. I trained as a computer engineer before an MSc in Finance, and I still build the data pipelines behind every piece of work myself.

The practice exists because the emerging-market questions asked in London were rarely answered well from London — least of all the India ones. Every engagement is scoped, researched and delivered by the analyst you speak to, which caps how much the practice takes on at once. The limit is deliberate.
