Two kinds of writing

They do different jobs, and they are held to different standards of length and claim.

  • Research

    Deep work. Longer structured papers with a defined question, a stated methodology and the data shown, written to be read closely and to hold up in front of a committee.

  • Briefings & data notes

    Short, dated pieces in Insights: what a data release, policy statement or market move actually changes. Narrower in claim, quicker to read. Read Insights →
