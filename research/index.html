How the research is done

Six stages · every source dated

The method

The comparison set is chosen, not inherited.

Every study begins by naming the alternatives the decision actually runs against, before a line of analysis is written. For the India work that has meant Indonesia, Brazil, Vietnam, Mexico, China or the whole classified EM set; for other questions it means a developed-market anchor, a peer group of companies or whichever comparators the question itself defines. Official data, filings and primary sources are pulled and cleaned in-house. Every figure carries its source and its date; judgement is labelled as judgement; and the conditions that would change a conclusion are stated beside it.

The same six stages run whether the work is a one-question memo, a research assignment or the flagship. Sources are recorded, the methodology is open to challenge, and corrections are published. Where the question is a decision to be argued for rather than a study to be delivered, it is scoped as a consulting mandate.
