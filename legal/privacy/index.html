Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kakde Research is an independent consulting and research practice based in India. This page describes what the practice does with the information you provide.

What is collected

The data collected through this site is limited to what you choose to submit:

  • Enquiry form: your name, work email, organisation, the service you are asking about and what you are trying to understand, plus — if you choose to give them — a timeframe, your role and your country. The form also records the page it was sent from and the referring address, so the practice knows how the enquiry arrived.
  • Research requests: your work email, optionally your name and organisation, and the report you asked for.
  • Mailing list: your email address.
  • Email: whatever you include when you write to the published address.

The site sets no advertising or profiling cookies and runs no third-party tracking scripts. Fonts, styles, scripts and images are served from this domain, so loading a page sends no request to any third party and nothing about your visit reaches another company.

Why it is used

Enquiry details are used to reply to you, to scope the work and to run the engagement if one follows. Mailing-list addresses are used only to send the monthly research note. Nothing submitted is sold, rented or shared for marketing, and no automated decisions are made about you.

Research requests: the work email you give is used to deliver the report you asked for and to keep a record of the request. It is not added to any mailing list unless you separately subscribe.

Enquiries and research requests are transmitted by the site’s hosting provider (Vercel), stored in a database service (Supabase) and delivered by a transactional email service (Resend), each acting on the practice’s instructions. No advertising or analytics scripts run on the site.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within twelve months. Research-request records are kept on the same basis as enquiries. Records relating to an engagement are kept for as long as the practice needs them for its own business and tax records. Mailing-list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your control over it

You can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Every mailing-list message includes an unsubscribe link. Requests go to inquiries@kakderesearch.com and are actioned within thirty days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
