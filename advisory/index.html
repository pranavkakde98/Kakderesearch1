Kakde Research / Advisory

Research for decisions that do not fit inside a standard report.

Client-specific research across India and emerging markets, built around one defined decision and the comparison set required to answer it. You bring the decision. We identify the markets, the evidence and the comparison it actually rests on — and decline the questions where independent research would not improve the call.

Questions clients bring

  • Should we be in this market at all, and what evidence would justify the commitment?

  • Our India allocation was set years ago. Does the case still hold against the alternatives?

  • The committee wants a view by next quarter. What should it actually say?

  • How does this market compare with the alternatives we already understand?

  • This regulation just changed. What does it do to our position?

  • Every pitch we see assumes the growth story. What if it is only half right?

  • We are close to committing. What have we missed?

Engagement formats

Seven shapes of work

Every format is a fixed-scope engagement: the question, the deliverable, the fee and the date agreed in writing before work starts. Projects are scoped individually — there is no off-the-shelf price list. Continuous specialist India coverage is the India Desk retainer, described on its own page.

01

Research Sprint

One specific question. Fast, focused, original research.

What it is

You bring a single defined question. You get a written answer with the reasoning and the data shown, built to be forwarded to your committee, not summarised from a call.

Who it is for

Investment offices, family offices and strategy teams that have a specific India or EM question and no internal owner for it.

What you receive

  • A 6-8 page written memo, with charts
  • Data appendix with sources
  • 45-minute walkthrough call and one written follow-up round

Typical window

5-7 working days.

What is excluded

No recommendation to buy or sell any security, and no personalised regulated investment advice. The memo gives you the analysis; the decision stays with you.

02

Bespoke Research Study

A substantial single-theme investigation, followed wherever the evidence goes.

What it is

A deeper study on a defined theme — a market’s investability, a sector, a currency regime, an ownership structure — followed across macro, policy and market structure and connected into one argument, with the comparison set named at the outset.

Who it is for

Allocators and strategy teams that need one theme understood properly rather than a broad survey that answers nothing precisely.

What you receive

  • A written study against the agreed scope, with charts
  • A complete, reproducible data appendix
  • Walkthrough call and one round of revision

Typical window

Scoped per project; fixed in writing before work starts.

What is excluded

No consensus summaries assembled from what other houses have written, and no guaranteed outcome. Research narrows uncertainty; it does not remove it.

03

Country Allocation Study

Comparative market work for CIOs and investment committees.

What it is

A like-for-like comparison of the markets competing for a defined allocation — India against Indonesia, Vietnam, Brazil, Mexico, the global anchors, or the set your committee actually debates — on the dimensions that decide the call: growth, valuation, currency, flows and investability.

Who it is for

CIO and multi-asset teams and committees choosing between markets, who need a comparison they can defend rather than a set of country brochures.

What you receive

  • A comparative study on a fixed peer set and metric set
  • Methodology stated openly enough to be challenged
  • Complete, reproducible data appendix and walkthrough call

Typical window

15 working days, or as scoped for a larger peer set.

What is excluded

No pre-cooked winner. Where a market ranks badly, the study says so first; no market wins the comparison by default.

04

Strategic Market Study

Private-capital and corporate questions, ahead of a commitment.

What it is

Market entry, expansion, sector or China+1 research on a defined scope — the evidence assembled first, the conclusion following it, whichever way it points, and the alternatives compared honestly rather than assumed away.

Who it is for

Corporates, strategy teams and private-capital investors weighing a specific commitment they will have to defend.

What you receive

  • A written study against the agreed scope
  • A risk register grounded in evidence, not generic caution
  • Sources and data appendix, and a walkthrough call

Typical window

Scoped per project; fixed in writing before work starts.

What is excluded

No guaranteed outcomes. If the question cannot be answered properly, that is said on the scoping call, before you have spent anything.

05

Investment Committee Support

Research designed around an upcoming capital-allocation decision.

What it is

Work built to land inside a specific committee cycle: the question your committee is about to decide, researched and written so the analysis, the comparison and the falsifier are all on the table before the vote.

Who it is for

Investment committees and the teams that staff them, who need an independent reading to test the internal case against.

What you receive

  • A written committee paper against the decision, with charts
  • The assumptions the position rests on, stated plainly enough to be challenged
  • A walkthrough call timed to your committee calendar

Typical window

Scoped to the committee date, fixed in writing.

What is excluded

Not a vote and not a recommendation to buy or sell. The paper states what the decision rests on; the decision stays with the committee.

06

Ongoing Research Advisory

Recurring access, updates, research calls and commissioned work.

What it is

A standing arrangement for organisations whose questions recur: priority access to the desk, periodic updates on the markets you hold, research calls, and commissioned pieces as decisions arise — without re-scoping the relationship each time.

Who it is for

Allocators and strategy teams with a continuing emerging-markets programme who need research capacity on call rather than a single study.

What you receive

  • Priority research capacity across India and EM
  • Periodic updates and research calls, scoped to your programme
  • Commissioned pieces as decisions arise

Typical window

A rolling arrangement, scoped in writing.

What is excluded

Not discretionary management or personalised regulated advice. The desk supplies research; allocation stays with you.

07

India Desk

Continuous specialist India coverage, as a monthly retainer.

What it is

Our deepest, most continuous coverage of a single market. A bespoke monthly retainer for organisations with meaningful India exposure and no dedicated India team — India read against the emerging markets competing for the same capital.

Who it is for

Asset managers, family offices, boutique investment firms and EM allocation teams for whom India is a standing question, not a one-off study.

What you receive

  • A monthly research note, rapid written Q&A and a monthly call
  • Comparative EM monitoring and maintained data
  • Priority research capacity — the full retainer is set out on the India Desk page

Typical window

A rolling monthly retainer.

How an engagement works

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    Thirty minutes to establish what decision is being made and what a useful answer would have to contain. If I don’t think your question can be answered properly, I will tell you on this call, before you have spent anything.

  2. 02

    Written scope, fixed date

    You receive the scope, the comparison set, the deliverable and the delivery date in writing. Nothing starts until it is agreed, and nothing is billed before it is.

  3. 03

    Research and analysis

    Data, primary sources, policy documents and market evidence, pulled and cleaned in-house and connected into one argument.

  4. 04

    Delivery and walkthrough

    A written report with the reasoning and the data shown, followed by a walkthrough call and one round of revision.

Every deliverable includes

  • A written report against a defined scope
  • The comparison set named and justified
  • Sources recorded and disclosed
  • A data appendix, where the analysis rests on data

Every process includes

  • A scope agreed in writing before work starts
  • A delivery discussion to walk through the findings
  • One round of revision, included

Independent research, not product sales.

The practice is independent: no external capital, no execution or brokerage business, nothing to distribute. Because research is the only thing on sale, the conclusions can afford to be inconvenient. In practical terms, that means:

  • It does not sell or distribute financial products.
  • It does not take custody of client assets.
  • It does not execute trades, or arrange for anyone else to.
  • It does not manufacture consensus. Where the evidence is unfavourable, the report says so first.
  • It does not promise outcomes. Research narrows uncertainty; it does not remove it.
  • It does not provide personalised, regulated investment advice. That requires a licensed adviser.

That leaves one thing to sell: the answer itself.
