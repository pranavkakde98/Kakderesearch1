Kakde Research / About / Founder

Pranav Prakash Kakde

Founder and lead researcher. He scopes every mandate, directs and reviews the research, and stays on the client relationship, working with a team of six analysts.

Pranav Prakash Kakde founded Kakde Research in 2025. He works from India, on mandates that reach well beyond it, with emerging markets and India the practice’s deepest specialist capability.

Before the practice he worked in London, at a global investment bank and a financial-data company, across macro and market data roles. The two jobs put the same problem in front of him from opposite ends: the demand for a reading of an emerging market specific enough to act on — India most insistently, but never India alone — and the data infrastructure any such reading depends on, how often it gets used without being understood.

He trained as a computer engineer before taking an MSc in Finance, and that order still shapes the work. The data pipelines behind every engagement are built and maintained in-house, not lifted from a vendor summary and re-described, which is why the appendix in a Kakde Research report can be rebuilt by the person reading it.

It makes the practice comparative by design: global capital decides between markets, not about them. The work follows the evidence across whichever markets a question names. India, the market he can read furthest on his own sources, is the deepest single coverage rather than the only one.

Where India ranks badly, he says so first. That is what makes the favourable findings worth anything.

Working with the team

Kakde Research is founder-led. Pranav Kakde scopes every mandate, directs and reviews the research, and stays on the client relationship; a team of six analysts assembles, checks and maintains the evidence. The people you speak to are the people doing the work, whether the engagement is a consulting project, a research assignment or an ongoing relationship. There is no account manager between the scoping call and the analysis that follows it.

Founder review on every deliverable caps how much runs at once, and the limit is deliberate. If a question cannot be answered properly within the time available, you will be told that on the scoping call, before you have spent anything.

Bring us the decision, question or assumption you need tested.

Describe what you are trying to understand, the markets it touches and what decision it relates to. We will get back to you as soon as possible.
