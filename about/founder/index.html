Kakde Research / About / Founder

Pranav Prakash Kakde

Founder and lead researcher. He scopes, researches and delivers every engagement.

Pranav Prakash Kakde founded Kakde Research in 2025. He works from India.

Before the practice he worked in London, at a global investment bank on the sell side and at a financial-data company, across macro and market data roles. The two jobs put the same problem in front of him from opposite ends: on one side the demand for a reading of India specific enough to act on, on the other the data infrastructure that any such reading depends on, and how often it gets used without being understood.

He trained as a computer engineer before taking an MSc in Finance. That order still shapes the work. The data pipelines behind every engagement are built and maintained in-house rather than lifted from a vendor summary and re-described, which is why the appendix in a Kakde Research report can be rebuilt by the person reading it.

The practice started from a narrow observation. The India questions asked in London were rarely answered well from London. The coverage available was either promotional, written to sell the market, or generic, written from the same secondary sources the client had already seen. What was missing was a local reading held to the standard of an institutional desk — first for India, and then for the emerging markets India is weighed against, which is where the comparative work now reaches.

Where India ranks badly, he says so first. That is what makes the favourable findings worth anything.

Working directly with the researcher

The researcher you speak to is the researcher who does the work. There is no account manager, no junior team, and no layer between the scoping call and the analysis that follows it.

That places a limit on how much work the practice can take at once, and the limit is deliberate. If a question cannot be answered properly within the time available, you will be told that on the scoping call, before you have spent anything.

Have a question worth investigating?

Describe what you are trying to understand and what decision it relates to. You will get a reply within two working days.
