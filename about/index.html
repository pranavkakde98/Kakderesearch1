About the practice

An independent emerging-markets research practice, based in India and founded in 2025. India is our deepest coverage market; emerging markets are the opportunity set. One analyst, no external capital, no execution business, and research fees as the only revenue.

What the practice is

Before starting Kakde Research I worked in London, at a global investment bank and at a financial-data company, across macro and market data. I trained as a computer engineer before taking an MSc in Finance, and I still build the data pipelines behind every piece of work myself.

The practice exists because of a gap I kept seeing from the other side: the emerging-market questions asked in London — and the India questions most of all — were rarely answered well from London. The coverage was either promotional, written to sell the market, or generic, written by someone reading the same secondary sources as the client. What was missing was a local reading with the discipline of an institutional desk. Based in India, researching emerging markets for global capital.

That is the work here. India is our deepest market, and it is never read in isolation: every conclusion is set against the emerging markets competing for the same capital — Indonesia, Vietnam, Brazil, Mexico — and the developed-market anchors an allocator already holds. You bring a defined question. You get a written answer with the reasoning and the data shown, built to be forwarded to your committee, not summarised from a call.

Why independent research

Independence gets claimed on most research websites. Here it is a matter of structure rather than assertion: nothing in the practice pays more when the conclusion goes one way rather than the other.

No products to sell

The practice sells written research and nothing else. There is no fund to market and no product shelf, so no finding has to lean the way a sale would need it to.

No execution business

There is no brokerage or execution arm attached to the work. Nothing here earns from whether you transact, which leaves the answer free to be ‘not yet’, or ‘no’.

No external capital

The practice is self-funded. There is no investor whose interests sit between the question you asked and the answer you get.

One line of revenue

Research fees are the only revenue. The practice grows only by being right often enough, and open enough about its reasoning, to be trusted with the next question.

Research philosophy

  • Independent thinking

    Consensus is a starting point to be tested, not a conclusion to be summarised. Every piece of work asks what the market is assuming, and what that assumption actually rests on.

  • Evidence first

    The position follows the data, not the other way round. Sources are pulled and cleaned in-house, recorded, and shown in the deliverable so the reasoning can be checked line by line.

  • Decision relevant

    Research earns its fee only if it changes what you do. Every engagement ends with what you should now understand differently, and what that means for the decision in front of you.
