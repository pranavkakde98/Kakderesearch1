Why the firm exists

Founded 2025 · Based in India · Working globally

The gap, seen from London

Emerging-market questions are asked everywhere. They are rarely answered well from a distance.

Before starting Kakde Research the founder worked in London, at a global investment bank and at a financial-data company, across macro and market data. He trained as a computer engineer before taking an MSc in Finance, and he still builds the data pipelines behind every piece of work himself. The gap was visible from both London seats: the emerging-market questions asked in London — and the India questions most of all — were rarely answered well from London. The coverage was either promotional, written to sell the market, or generic, written from the same secondary sources the client had already read. What was missing was a reading close to the market, held to the discipline of an institutional desk.

The practice was founded in 2025 to close that gap, and it is comparative by design. Global capital does not decide whether a market is attractive; it decides whether that market is more attractive than the alternatives competing for the same allocation, the same plant or the same customer. So every conclusion here is set against the peers the question actually names — India against Indonesia, Vietnam, Brazil and Mexico; a relocation thesis across the whole China+1 set; an external-accounts question across the commodity exporters — and against the developed-market anchors an allocator already holds. A market conclusion that cannot survive contact with the alternatives is a description, not a finding.

Research-led consulting and the direct researcher model

How the firm is built

Research-led

Consulting here starts from research, not from a framework. Every project — a market comparison, an entry study, a value-chain assessment, a decision memo — is built from sourced series, filings and primary documents, and the deliverable shows the evidence and the reasoning so your own team can check it line by line. The practice does not sell a conclusion. It sells the work that supports one.

Direct researcher

There is one researcher here, and you deal with him directly: no account manager, no junior team and no layer between the scoping conversation and the analysis that follows it. That caps how much the practice takes on at once, and the limit is deliberate: it is what keeps the person who knows your context on the work.

India depth

India is the firm’s deepest specialist market: macro, the RBI, fiscal policy, banking and credit, capital flows, corporate earnings, ownership, valuation and market structure, read from India and followed continuously. It is the market on which the practice goes furthest on its own sources, and the one it is never asked to explain from a distance.

Broader EM capability

The same discipline runs across the emerging markets that compete with one another for capital and plant — Indonesia, Vietnam, China, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa and the others a question names. The comparative frame is the firm-wide capability. India is where it goes deepest, not where it stops.

Independence

No brokerage, no execution, no product distribution, no asset-management incentive and no external capital. No part of the practice has a stake in which way a finding goes.

Source transparency

Every figure can be traced to its origin, with its series, observation date and retrieval date. Where a series has been revised or interrupted, that is recorded rather than smoothed over. Judgement is labelled as judgement, and the conditions that would change a conclusion are stated alongside it.

Independence, in practice

Structure, not assertion

Independence gets claimed on most research websites. Here it is a matter of structure rather than assertion: nothing in the practice pays more when the conclusion goes one way rather than the other.

No products to sell

The practice sells research and consulting and nothing else. There is no fund to market and no product shelf, so no finding has to lean the way a sale would need it to.

No execution business

There is no brokerage or execution arm attached to the work. Nothing here earns from whether you transact, which leaves the answer free to be ‘not yet’, or ‘no’.

No external capital

The practice is self-funded. There is no investor whose interests sit between the question you asked and the answer you get.

One line of revenue

Research and consulting fees are the only revenue. The practice grows only by being right often enough, and open enough about its reasoning, to be trusted with the next question.
