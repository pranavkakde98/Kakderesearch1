About the practice

Kakde Research is an independent consulting and research firm helping investors, businesses and advisers evaluate opportunities, test assumptions and make strategic decisions. We work across companies, industries and markets through individual projects and ongoing retained relationships. Emerging markets and India are areas of particular depth within the practice.

Why the firm exists

Founded 2025 · Based in India · Working globally

The client’s question

Built around the decision.

Important decisions usually depend on a few questions that broad market coverage cannot settle. Is the opportunity as attractive as it appears? Which assumptions hold up? What would change the case for proceeding?

Kakde Research works through those questions with the people responsible for the decision. We define the brief, investigate the evidence and explain what the findings mean for the options available. The work can concern a single company, an industry, a market or several alternatives.

Clients can engage us for a specific assignment or retain us as questions develop. In either case, they work directly with the people carrying out the analysis.

Research-led, founder-led and independent

How the firm is built

Research-led

We examine the evidence behind a decision: company filings, industry economics, official data and the documents relevant to the question. Findings show how the analysis was built, which assumptions matter and where the evidence remains incomplete.

Founder-led team

Pranav leads the engagement and reviews the findings. The analyst team develops and checks the supporting research, with responsibilities agreed around the assignment. Clients have direct access to the people working on their question throughout the engagement.

Depth in emerging markets and India

Two specialist desks provide detailed coverage of emerging markets and India. Their work connects economic and policy developments with industry structure, corporate earnings, financing, ownership and market access. Both support focused assignments and continuing relationships.

Across markets

An engagement can focus on one company, one industry or one country, or compare several alternatives. A US business, a European industry or a Canadian market can be the whole brief. We agree the coverage around the question, the expertise it requires and the evidence available.

Independence

No brokerage, no execution, no product distribution, no asset-management incentive and no external capital. No part of the practice has a stake in which way a finding goes.

Source transparency

Every figure can be traced to its origin, with its series, observation date and retrieval date. Where a series has been revised or interrupted, that is recorded rather than smoothed over. Judgement is labelled as judgement, and the conditions that would change a conclusion are stated alongside it.

Independence, in practice

Structure, not assertion

Independence gets claimed on most research websites. Here it is a matter of structure rather than assertion: nothing in the practice pays more when the conclusion goes one way rather than the other.

No products to sell

The practice sells research and consulting and nothing else. There is no fund to market and no product shelf, so no finding has to lean the way a sale would need it to.

No execution business

There is no brokerage or execution arm attached to the work. Nothing here earns from whether you transact, which leaves the answer free to be ‘not yet’, or ‘no’.

No external capital

The practice is self-funded. There is no investor whose interests sit between the question you asked and the answer you get.

One line of revenue

Research and consulting fees are the only revenue. The practice grows only by being right often enough, and open enough about its reasoning, to be trusted with the next question.
