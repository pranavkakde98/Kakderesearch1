Retained research service · Monthly

Your external emerging-markets research desk.

A retained comparative-research service for organisations that make recurring decisions across emerging markets and do not want to staff a full research team to do it. You and Kakde Research agree the covered markets, the questions, the cadence, the response times and the exclusions in writing — and the desk maintains that universe.

Discuss an Emerging Markets Desk

The boundary

A defined universe, maintained — not a promise to watch every market at once.

The desk supports recurring decisions across the market universe you agree at the outset. That is a real commercial boundary, and it is stated plainly: the desk maintains the client-defined markets in scope, rather than implying permanent on-the-ground coverage of every emerging economy. The universe can be broad or focused, and it can change by agreement as your mandate does.

The analytical centre is yours to set. The current flagship study centres India because India is its question; an Emerging Markets Desk engagement can centre Indonesia, Vietnam, China, Brazil or another market and select the relevant comparators around it. This is not “India plus peers” — it is one method applied to whichever market your decisions turn on.

A sample operating model

Illustrative scope · fixed in the engagement proposal

One shape a desk can take. The proposal defines the number of markets, the eligible quick-turn questions, the call cadence, the response time, the deep-dive allowance, the data sources and what falls out of scope. Not every desk carries every element.

01 · Monthly comparative note

A research note each month on a cross-market question within your universe — the decision the month actually raised, answered comparatively.

02 · Maintained scorecard

A country or opportunity-set scorecard across the agreed markets, refreshed as the sources release, with each figure sourced and dated.

03 · Rapid written Q&A

Short-turnaround written answers to eligible questions inside the agreed service boundary and response-time SLA.

04 · Event-driven briefs

Policy, currency, market-structure or macro briefs when an event in the covered universe changes a decision.

05 · Monthly discussion

A structured call with the analyst who does the work — the note, the scorecard and whatever the month raised.

06 · Periodic deep dive

A larger question graduates into a dedicated study within an agreed allowance, scoped like any project.

Who it is for, and what it decides

Recurring cross-market decisions

Who it is for

Allocators with a standing EM programme

Global and EM asset managers, CIO and multi-asset teams, and sophisticated family offices weighing markets against one another rather than in isolation — and corporate strategy teams comparing markets for capital or footprint decisions.

Decisions it supports

Where the marginal dollar or the next move goes

Relative country and sector positioning, funding one market from another, sizing a position to a mandate, and reading a global shock — the dollar, rates, oil, trade — through its effect on the markets you hold.

Sample questions the desk answers

  • For a fixed EM budget, does the next unit belong in India, Indonesia or a return to China — and what would flip the ranking?
  • Which market’s valuation premium is supported by earnings, and which is a story the market has agreed to keep telling?
  • What does a weaker dollar change across the currencies and valuations in our universe?
  • India, Vietnam or Mexico for the China+1 build-out — and who captures the value in each?
  • What does sustained US-rate pressure mean for the markets we hold?

Sample deliverables

A monthly comparative note; a maintained cross-market scorecard; dated written answers to in-scope questions; event-driven briefs; and periodic deep-dive studies — each with sources shown and judgement labelled as judgement. These are illustrative formats, not a record of client work.

How scope is set — and what it excludes

Everything agreed in writing first

Defined in the proposal

  • The covered market universe
  • Eligible quick-turn questions and the response-time SLA
  • Call cadence and deep-dive allowance
  • Data sources and refresh cadence

Out of scope

  • Markets outside the agreed universe, until re-scoped
  • Asset management, execution or product distribution
  • Personalised, regulated investment advice
  • Any promise of a market outcome
